Towers — card art style mockups

Same four cards (Basic Wall · Earthquake · Prism · Faery) in four SVG styles, inside the real card frame. All vector, all in-repo, all recolorable.

Style 1 — Flat geometric

Flat shapes, no outlines, muted per-color backdrops. Cheap to author, scales to all ~50 cards fast, reads clearly at the tiny in-hand size.

Style 4 — Pixel art

Chunky retro sprites (rendered here from character maps into SVG rects). Strong nostalgic Arcomage vibe; art is literally a text grid, so it's very easy to tweak.
